Questions

Before you start.

More on Help and policies.

Who answers my questions, an AI or a person?

Two AI roles answer straight away: the Health-check Advisor for packages and booking, and the Report Explainer for your report. Each answer shows which one replied. A person on our team confirms appointments and quotations, and takes over whenever you ask.

Can I ask in Thai?

Yes. Answers follow the language you write in, and the website is in Thai by default; switch to English with the TH/EN control at the top.

Is this a real clinic?

No. LabClear is a coursework business simulation. Packages, prices and centers are simulated and no clinical service takes place.

Is my appointment booked straight away?

Your request holds the slot until a team member confirms or declines it. {{ policies.cancellation_policy }}

How do payments work here?

{{ policies.payment_policy }}

Can it tell me whether I am ill?

No. It explains what a test measures and how your confirmed value compares with the range printed on your report, with sources. Diagnosis and treatment belong with a clinician.

Can I upload a report from another laboratory?

Yes. The reader takes JPEG, PNG or PDF files up to 3 MB. It reads test rows only, not names, IDs or addresses, and you confirm every value before it is used.

Who can see my report?

Only you. Staff see how many reports you have, never the values. Deleting a report also clears conversation history that could contain its values.

What is free?

One AI reading of one report image, the Lab Report for it, questions with sources, and every synthetic sample report.

What does Plus add?

For {{ money(plus.price_thb) }} and {{ plus.period_days }} days: readings without the one-report limit, up to {{ plus.limits.images_per_read }} pages or images at once, your results over time and the change since your previous report.

Does Plus renew automatically?

No. It ends after {{ plus.period_days }} days. You can renew in the last 7 days and keep the remaining days. In this release every payment is a test payment.
