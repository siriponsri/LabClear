Privacy

Coursework privacy notice. It describes how this software behaves; it is not a legal compliance statement.

Use synthetic data only

{{ policies.privacy_policy }}

What is stored

  • Account email and a salted password hash. Sessions use an HTTP-only cookie.
  • Conversations, reports you upload, appointments, quotations, test payments and notifications, encrypted in the application database.
  • An audit trail of actions such as confirmations and refunds, without message contents.

Who can see it

Your reports and conversations are private to your account. Staff see a conversation when you ask for the team or when an organization request needs coordination. Organizations never receive individual results.

When an AI provider is connected, the minimum text needed for a reply is sent to that provider. Reports are never added to the shared knowledge base.

Your controls

Delete a report from My reports; this also clears conversation history that may contain its values. Sign out from your account menu. Email verification and account recovery are not available in this coursework release.
