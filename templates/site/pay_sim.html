Test payment simulator

This page simulates a payment provider for coursework. It cannot take real money. Never enter card numbers or bank details here.

How the simulator works

Each button sends a signed test event to LabClear, the same way a payment provider would notify a real shop. The server checks the signature, amount, currency and order before changing anything.

Failed, expired or cancelled payments leave the appointment unpaid, and you can start again or pay at the center.
